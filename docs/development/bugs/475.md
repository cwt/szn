# Bug 475 — Uncapped server-to-client frame can exceed the client packet cap

### 475. Asymmetric packet caps between directions
**Severity:** MEDIUM
**Status:** Open
**Files:** [`src/server/dispatch.zig`](../../../src/server/dispatch.zig), [`src/server/protocol.zig`](../../../src/server/protocol.zig), [`src/main.zig`](../../../src/main.zig)

---

### Root Cause Analysis

`validPacketLength` caps at 16 MiB, `MessageReader` caps client→server at 8 KiB, but `sendResponse` emits `server.response_buf` uncapped. A large `capture-pane`/sixel frame `>16MiB` trips the client guard at [`main.zig:921`](../../../src/main.zig#L921) ("oversized", exit).

### Proposed Fix

Chunk or cap server→client frames at the same 16 MiB limit (truncate with a warning, or split `capture-pane`/sixel output across packets) so the client never receives a frame it must drop.
