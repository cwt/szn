# Bug 540 — status bar remnant on exit

### 540. Status bar remnant persists on screen after szn exits (#539 follow-up)
**Severity:** MEDIUM
**Status:** ✅ FIXED
**Files:** [`src/main.zig`](https://github.com/cwt/szn/blob/main/src/main.zig), [`src/server/render.zig`](https://github.com/cwt/szn/blob/main/src/server/render.zig)

## Problem

After the [#539](539.md) fix, exiting `szn` still left a remnant of the status bar painted at the bottom of the screen. Detailed investigation uncovered three contributing defects:

1. **Duplicate `display.exitAltScreen()` execution (Commit `344b7f9` / rev 782)**:
   When moving the stdout drain and `exitAltScreen()` into a `defer` block, the existing `defer display.exitAltScreen() catch ...;` on line 702 was not removed. Because Zig executes defers in LIFO order, the inner defer drained `out_buf` and called `exitAltScreen()`, and then the outer defer called `exitAltScreen()` a second time. This sent duplicate `\x1b[<1u` and `\x1b[?1049l` sequences, corrupting keyboard protocol pop counts and restoring the cursor on the primary screen twice without a matching DECSC.

2. **Missing SGR reset and display clear in `Display.exitAltScreen`**:
   `Display.exitAltScreen` sent only `\x1b[?7h\x1b[<1u\x1b[?1000l\x1b[?1002l\x1b[?1006l\x1b[?2004l\x1b[?25h\x1b[?1049l`.
   Unlike upstream tmux (which emits `\x1b[m\x1b[H\x1b[2J...\x1b[?1049l`), it lacked:
   - `\x1b[m` (SGR reset): The status bar applies background colours (e.g. `\x1b[48;2;...m`); without reset, text rendition attributes bleed into subsequent terminal output.
   - `\x1b[H\x1b[2J` (home + erase display): For terminal environments that do not isolate alternate screen buffers cleanly (or where alternate screen is disabled/unsupported), omitting display clear leaves the status bar intact on row `sy`.
   - `\x1b[?2026l` (synchronized update end): Any pending synchronized frame was left unterminated.

3. **Non-blocking `stdout_fd` during `enterAltScreen()`**:
   `setNonBlocking(stdout_fd)` was called before `display.enterAltScreen()`. If `writeBytes` hit `EAGAIN` during startup, `enterAltScreen()` failed silently, causing szn to render entirely on the primary screen buffer where its status bar remained permanently after exit.

## Fix

1. **`src/main.zig`**:
   - Keep `stdout_fd` blocking until after `display.enterAltScreen()` completes.
   - Remove the duplicate outer `defer display.exitAltScreen()`.
   - Use a single, unified `defer` block that restores blocking mode, drains `out_buf`, and runs `display.exitAltScreen()`.
   - Activate `setNonBlocking(stdout_fd)` only after alternate screen entry.

2. **`src/server/render.zig`**:
   - Update `Display.exitAltScreen` to emit `\x1b[m\x1b[?2026l\x1b[H\x1b[2J\x1b[?7h\x1b[<1u\x1b[?1000l\x1b[?1002l\x1b[?1006l\x1b[?2004l\x1b[?25h\x1b[?1049l`, matching upstream tmux behaviour.
   - Add unit test verifying `enterAltScreen` and `exitAltScreen` byte sequences.
