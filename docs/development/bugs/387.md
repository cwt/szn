# Bug 387 — Sixel anchor/state tracking defects

### 387. Three related overlay-tracking bugs
**File:** `src/server/render.zig:818-819` (`const state_count = @min(bounds.len, 8); var states: [8]...`),
`src/screen.zig:1152-1175` (SU), `src/screen.zig:1192-1211` (SD)
**Severity:** LOW
**Status:** ✅ FIXED

### Fix (applied)
1. Region SU now shifts sixel anchors by the full scroll amount
   (`shiftSixelAnchors(-count)`) instead of once for an n-line scroll.
2. Region SD gained the missing `shiftSixelAnchors(+count)` call, mirroring
   the non-region branch.
3. Render per-pane state cap raised 8 → `MAX_RENDER_PANES = 32` with a comment
   marking it a degenerate-layout bound; panes 9..32 now draw and erase their
   images correctly.

**Test:** `region scroll shifts sixel anchors by full amount — bug #387`
(4-line region SU moves anchor by 4; region SD shifts back down).

### Problem
1. With more than 8 panes, images in panes 9+ are neither drawn nor erased,
   and their stale `sixel_last_anchor` entries keep forcing `needs_erase`
   every frame (wasted work + invisible sixels).
2. Region SU scrolls n>1 lines but shifts sixel anchors by exactly one row
   (call sits outside the per-line loop).
3. Region SD branch has no compensating `shiftSixelAnchors(+1)` although the
   non-region branch does (:1218).

Images anchored inside regions desync from their marker cells after any
multi-line region scroll.

### Fix (proposed)
Allocate per-pane state dynamically (or iterate bounds directly), hoist the
anchor shift into the scroll amount (`shiftSixelAnchors(-@as(i32,@intCast(n)))`)
and add the missing SD counterpart.
