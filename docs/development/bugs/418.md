# Bug 418 — Detached clients keep a live control channel

### 418. Detached clients keep a live control channel: after .detach the fd stays in client_fds and can still inject stdin/commands
**Severity:** LOW
**Status:** ✅ RESOLVED

The `.detach` packet branch removes the entry from `display_clients` only. The socket remains registered in `client_fds` with its `MessageReader` live: `.stdin_data` (:2496-2498) and `.command` from that socket keep acting on the global active session — keystroke injection, `kill-session`, everything — until the peer closes. tmux kills the detached client process; szn's detach is advisory and leaves the channel open.

**Fix:** on detach completion, close the connection (client's contract is exit-on-detach, cf. main.zig handling) or gate `handleClient` to `.detach`/`.redraw` for retired display clients.
