### 368. LF/IND scroll-region bypass
**File:** `src/screen.zig` (LF/IND handling), `src/grid.zig` (scrollUp)
**Severity:** HIGH
**Status:** ✅ FIXED

### Fix (applied)
`Screen.index()` now short-circuits whenever a scroll region is active:
- cursor at the region bottom → `scrollUpInRegion()` (unchanged);
- cursor below the region → moves down but clamps at the screen bottom,
  never falling through to the whole-grid `grid.scrollUp()` that pushed
  out-of-region lines into scrollback;
- no region → original behaviour.

**Tests:** `LF below scroll region clamps without whole-grid scroll — bug
#368` (row content survives, no history push, downward move below region);
`LF at region bottom scrolls only the region — bug #368`.

### Problem
When DECSTBM margins are active and the cursor sits below/at the bottom of the
region but above the screen bottom, line-feed/index must scroll only within the
region. The audit sweep identified paths where LF/IND bypass the region check
and drive whole-grid scrolling — lines from outside the region get pushed into
scrollback and region content desyncs. User-visible daily in apps that use
scroll regions (editors, htop).

### Fix (proposed)
Route every cursor-advance-newline through one helper that honours
`origin_mode + region`; add table-driven tests for LF at each boundary
combination (inside/below region × origin on/off).
