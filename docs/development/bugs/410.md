# Bug 410 — BufferList.delete's swapRemove breaks the newest-at-0 / oldest-at-last positional contract that get(null) and evictOldestIfFull depend on

### 410. BufferList.delete's swapRemove breaks the newest-at-0 / oldest-at-last positional contract that get(null) and evictOldestIfFull depend on
**Severity:** MEDIUM
**Status:** ✅ RESOLVED

The list maintains recency **by position**: pushes `insert(..., 0, ...)` (newest at index 0), `get(null)` reads `items[0]`, `evictOldestIfFull` drops `items[len-1]`. `delete` uses `swapRemove(i)` (:67), which moves the **last (oldest) entry into the deleted slot**:

- `delete-buffer` on the newest buffer → the oldest becomes `items[0]` → a bare `paste-buffer` (cmdDeleteBuffer/cmdPasteBuffer, no `-b`) now pastes/deletes the *oldest* buffer;
- the next push at capacity evicts the wrongly-oldest-positioned entry (possibly the newest).

Also `generateName` returns `name_buf[0..0]` — an **empty name** — after 10 000 collisions (:93), which then aliases with any other empty-name lookup/delete.

**Fix:** `orderedRemove` (O(n) over ≤50 entries, trivial); return an error from `generateName` on exhaustion.
